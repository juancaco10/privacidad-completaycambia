Privacy Policy

Album Tracker — JC Papelería e Informática · Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Who we are

"Album Tracker" is an Android app that lets you manage your collection of collectible stickers (World Cups, Copa América and other albums) and arrange swaps with other collectors. On Google Play it appears as "Sticker Album: World Cup" (English), "Álbum de Figuritas Mundial" (Spanish) and "Figurinhas e Álbum da Copa" (Portuguese). The data controller is JC Papelería e Informática (contact: jcpapeleriaeinformatica@gmail.com).

This app is independent: it is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Panini, FIFA or CONMEBOL. Those names are used only to identify albums.

We process your data under the data protection laws that apply depending on where you are (for example Uruguay's Law 18.331, the EU GDPR and Brazil's LGPD).

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app:

  • Guest mode (anonymous): we generate a unique anonymous identifier (Firebase Anonymous UID) and a random installation ID. We do not collect your name, email or other identifying personal data.
  • Sign in with Google: we receive your display name, email address and profile photo. Your email is stored on our server so we can recover your account if Google assigns you a different identifier, and as a contact option if you allow it (see 2.2).

In both cases we store:

  • Your sticker collection (owned, missing and duplicates) for each album you add.
  • Your preferences (language, light/dark theme, favorite team, avatar frame and notification preferences).
  • Your achievements and experience points (XP) inside the app.
  • Your PRO plan status, if you have one.

2.1 Public location (optional)

For the swap feature you can type your country, state/province and city. The app does not use GPS or the device location: only what you enter. This information is visible to other users in listings and on the swap screen, and is used only to suggest nearby people with complementary stickers. If you do not want to appear, leave it empty.

2.2 Contact channel (optional)

You can add a WhatsApp or Telegram contact; it is stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM and never shown on public screens. If you sign in with Google, your Google email is enabled by default as a way to contact you. In both cases the other person only sees your contact if you expressly accept a swap request. You can turn it off, change it or delete it at any time in "Profile → Privacy and contact".

2.3 Listings, requests, connections and blocks

We store the listings you create (album, location and which contact options you offer), the contact requests you send and receive with their status, the resulting connections and the users you block. A blocked user cannot contact you or appear in your results, and any existing contact connection is cut.

2.4 Instant swaps by QR code

When you scan another person's QR code, both collections are compared to show possible matches, and if both confirm, each collection is updated. We keep a swap history (stickers, with whom and when). This does not share your location or contact channel: only the sticker codes.

2.5 PRO purchases (in-app)

The PRO plan can be a subscription (monthly or annual) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing: we never see or store your card or payment data. We verify the purchase with Google and store an irreversible hash of the receipt, plus the status and expiry date, to enable the benefits and to prevent one receipt from being used on several accounts. If you buy the lifetime plan, any subscription you already had is not cancelled automatically: you must cancel it yourself in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

2.6 Reports and feedback

If you use "Report a problem" we store your message, the category, your email if you choose to include it, the app version and the platform.

2.7 Technical data

The server temporarily logs your IP address (up to 24 hours) to limit abuse, and keeps error logs with internal identifiers. With your consent, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports (see section 4).

2.8 What we do NOT collect

We do not access your contacts, SMS, microphone, photo gallery or GPS location. We do not sell your data.

3. How we use data

  • To save and sync your collection, albums and preferences across devices.
  • To suggest people with complementary stickers by country, region and city.
  • To show your contact only when both sides accept a request.
  • To process QR swaps and keep their history.
  • To enable PRO benefits according to the status confirmed by Google Play.
  • To send push notifications (if you grant permission) about requests, acceptances and nearby listings; you can turn off each type.
  • To show ads, diagnose errors, prevent abuse and improve the app.

4. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We use these providers, only for the purposes listed:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): authentication and push notifications (FCM). May process data outside your country, including the United States.
  • Google Sign-In: to sign in with your Google account.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): crash reports (traces, device model, Android version, installation identifier). Only enabled if you consent.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): shows ads. It may collect the Android advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device information and ad events. Through Google's consent form (UMP) you choose whether ads are personalized; you can change it in "Privacy options". PRO users see no ads. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): processes payments; we only receive the purchase status.
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition): reads sticker codes on your device; camera images are not stored or sent to our servers.
  • Own server (jcpapeleria.com.es): syncs profile, collection, albums, listings, swaps, connections and notification token. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is hosted with Hostinger, whose servers may be outside your country of residence.

Other users only see: your display name, your profile photo (if uploaded), your public location (if provided) and which type of contact you offer (not its value). The real value is revealed only after mutual acceptance.

5. Permissions we request

  • Camera: to scan stickers (text) and QR codes. Optional; the app works without it.
  • Notifications: for requests, acceptances and news. You can deny it.
  • Internet: to sync and connect with other collectors.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by AdMob for ads.
  • Google Play Billing: for PRO plan purchases.

6. Artificial intelligence

  • In the app: we do not use generative AI and we do not send your data to AI services. The scanner's text recognition (Google ML Kit) runs on your device.
  • In development: for transparency, AI assistants (Claude, by Anthropic) were used during development, testing and documentation to write and review code and text. No user data is given to them; the developer reviews and tests the changes.
  • In the store: some graphics or texts in the Google Play listing may have been created or edited with AI help and are declared as such where applicable.

7. Storage and security

  • Data travels encrypted over HTTPS and access to servers is restricted.
  • Your WhatsApp or Telegram contact is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM before being stored.
  • Sensitive device data (session tokens) is stored with Android's secure storage (Keystore).
  • We apply usage limits to prevent abuse and review server and app vulnerabilities regularly.

No system is 100% secure. If we detect a breach affecting your data we will tell you and notify the competent authority where required.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: side menu → "Delete account". It immediately deletes your account, your server data and your authentication user.
  • On the web: account deletion page.
  • By email to jcpapeleriaeinformatica@gmail.com; we reply within 72 business hours.

Deletion removes profile, collection, albums, listings, contact channel, requests, connections, blocks, swap history, achievements, notification token and submitted reports. Server backups are kept for up to 14 days and your account data is also removed from them when you delete it. Temporary IP addresses are discarded within 24 hours. Google keeps purchase records under its own policies.

9. Legal basis

  • Your consent (e.g. GDPR art. 6.1.a) for personalized ads, crash reports, notifications, public location and contact.
  • Performance of the service (GDPR art. 6.1.b) for the account, sync and purchases.
  • Our legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6.1.f) in service security and abuse prevention.

You can withdraw consent at any time from the app ("Privacy options" and "Profile → Privacy and contact") or by deleting your account. Data may be transferred outside your country (for example to the United States, through Google services) with the safeguards those providers offer.

10. Children

The app may be used by the general public, but the features for swapping with other people (posting, contacting) are intended for people aged 13 and over. We recommend that minors use them with adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

You can access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data, and withdraw your consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country (in Uruguay, the URCDP). To exercise your rights write to jcpapeleriaeinformatica@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. For important changes we will notify you in the app. The last-updated date is always at the top.
