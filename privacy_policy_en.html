Privacy Policy

Falta Menos — JC Papelería e Informática · Last updated: May 21, 2026

1. Who we are

"Falta Menos" is an Android mobile application that allows users to manage their 2026 World Cup sticker collection and coordinate trades with other collectors. The data controller is JC Papelería e Informática.

2. Data we collect

Depending on your access method:

  • Guest mode (anonymous): we generate a unique anonymous identifier (Firebase Anonymous UID). We do not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Google Sign-In: we collect your display name, email address, and profile picture provided by your Google account, exclusively to identify you within the app and personalize your experience.

In both cases, we store:

  • Your sticker collection (owned, missing, and duplicates) and the active album if you manage multiple ones.
  • App preferences (language, dark/light theme, favorite team, avatar frame).

2.1 Public location data (optional)

To use the trading module, you can provide your country, state/province, and city. This data is public to other app users within the matches screen and is used solely to suggest nearby people with complementary stickers. If you do not wish to appear in matches, do not provide your location and the module will remain disabled for your account.

2.2 Private contact channel (optional, encrypted)

You may optionally provide a contact channel (WhatsApp, email, or Telegram) which the other person will only see if you expressly accept a trade request. This value is stored encrypted on our server using AES-256-GCM and is never displayed on the public matches screen. You can delete or replace it at any time from "Profile → Privacy & contact".

2.3 Requests, connections, and blocks

We log the contact requests you send and receive, their status (pending, accepted, rejected, ignored, expired), the resulting connections, and the users you block. Blocked users cannot send you new requests or appear in your matches.

3. How we use data

  • To save and synchronize your collection and preferences across devices.
  • To suggest people with complementary stickers to your collection (matchmaking by country/region/city and collection difference).
  • To reveal your private contact channel only when both parties accept a request.
  • To send you push notifications related to trades (only if you grant permission).
  • To improve the app's performance and diagnose errors.

4. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data solely with the following providers and for the stated purposes:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): authentication, data storage, and push notifications (FCM). Firebase may process data on servers located outside your country, including the United States.
  • Google Sign-In: for logging in with your Google account.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): collects crash reports (stack traces, device model, Android version, anonymous installation identifier) for diagnostics. It is activated only after your consent.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): displays ads within the app. AdMob may collect the Android advertising identifier (AAID), IP address, device information, and ad events. If you do not grant consent, ads will be served in non-personalized mode (NPA). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Our own backend (jcpapeleria.com.es): our server synchronizes your profile, collection, trades, connections, and FCM token. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on Hostinger servers located in the European Union.

Other app users can only see: your display name, your profile picture (if provided), your public location (country/region/city if provided), and the type of contact channel you offer (WhatsApp/email/Telegram, not the actual value). The real value of the channel is only revealed upon mutual acceptance.

5. Requested permissions

  • Camera: solely to scan stickers with Optical Character Recognition (OCR). Images are processed locally on your device and are neither stored nor sent to any server.
  • Notifications: to alert you when you receive a request or when someone accepts yours (you can deny this without affecting the rest of the app).
  • Internet: to synchronize your collection and connect with other collectors.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by AdMob to serve ads.

6. Storage and security

Data travels encrypted via HTTPS and is stored on restricted-access servers in the European Union (Hostinger). We apply the following additional measures:

  • Private contact channel (WhatsApp / email / Telegram) is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM before being stored in the database. Encryption keys are stored separately in server environment variables and rotated periodically.
  • Sensitive device data (session tokens, refresh tokens) are stored using Android's secure storage (Keystore).
  • Rate limiting on the backend to prevent abuse and enumeration.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data as long as your account is active. You can delete it at any time in three ways:

  • From the app's side menu: "Delete account" — immediately deletes your account and all your data from the server.
  • From the web form: https://jcpapeleria.com.es/delete-account/
  • By emailing jcpapeleriaeinformatica@gmail.com requesting deletion.

Deletion removes your profile, collection, albums, encrypted contact channel, sent/received requests, connections, blocks, notification token, and backend logs. Some data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before permanent purging.

8. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

The legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Your consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) for personalized advertising, crash analytics, push notifications, public location, and private contact channel.
  • The performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) for the account, collection synchronization, and core functionality.

You can withdraw your consent at any time from the app menu (Profile → Privacy & contact) or by deleting your account.

9. Minors

The application can be used by the general public. We do not intentionally collect personal data from children without parental or guardian consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with data without your consent, contact us to have it deleted.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, object to processing, restrict processing, and port your data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us by email at jcpapeleriaeinformatica@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you via the app of significant changes. The date of the last update will always be visible at the beginning of this document.
